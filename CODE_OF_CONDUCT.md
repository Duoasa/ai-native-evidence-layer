# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md) | English

## Our standard

We commit to a respectful, evidence-oriented, and harassment-free community. Productive participation includes:

- criticizing claims and designs without attacking people;
- distinguishing facts, interpretations, proposals, and uncertainty;
- giving sources and correcting mistakes openly;
- respecting privacy, attribution, licensing, and contributor boundaries;
- making space for different languages, backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, plagiarism, repeated disruption, or retaliation against good-faith reporting.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in public or private project-related communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact a maintainer through a private channel listed on their verified GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive personal details in an issue. Maintainers may warn, remove content, restrict participation, or ban a participant in proportion to impact and repeated behavior.

Enforcement decisions should document the violated standard and action while minimizing disclosure. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves when another qualified reviewer is available.
