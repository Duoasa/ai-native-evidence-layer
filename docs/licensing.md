# Licensing Policy

[简体中文](licensing.zh-CN.md) | English

## Project-authored material

Original code, schemas, tests, and documentation in this repository are licensed under Apache License 2.0 unless a file states otherwise.

## Catalog data

The public data dedication or license for project-authored catalog metadata is intentionally undecided until the first real pilot's source terms are reviewed. No generated pilot dataset should be advertised as openly relicensable before that decision is recorded.

## Third-party evidence

- A public URL does not erase copyright, database rights, terms, privacy, or attribution obligations.
- References identify sources; they do not relicense source content.
- Keep quotations minimal and necessary for verification.
- Store source license or terms metadata when relevant and known.
- Remove or quarantine material when rights are unclear and publication is not necessary to validate the protocol.

## Contributions

Unless explicitly marked otherwise before submission, intentionally submitted project contributions are accepted under Apache License 2.0 as described by that license. Contributors must have the right to submit their material.

This policy is an engineering boundary, not legal advice. Material commercial or cross-jurisdictional data use requires appropriate legal review.
